A board written here keeps nothing of what it had: an image carries its own bootloader and partition table. The images that ship with the site are in bin/, and a file of your own is just as welcome.

The other two are here because they belong to the same half-hour: a board that has just been written is the one about to be put on a network, and the one most likely to be asked why a sensor says nothing — where the answer is usually the wiring, the address or the bus speed rather than the firmware.
