What The Two Signals Mean

The legacy signal is the absolute field measured at one pixel. It includes the magnet's steady field and any stray field, so its direction can move when the environment changes.

The differential signal compares neighboring pixels. Uniform external fields tend to cancel, while the local field gradient from the magnet remains. It is useful for position and direction, but it is not the same unit or scale as the legacy field.

Why The Radar Target Can Disappear

In simulation, the radar has a known mechanical target. With live hardware, this page has sensor measurements but no independent position reference, so it hides the simulated target instead of pretending the comparison is calibrated.

This keeps the measured vector contained in the radar and makes the diagnostic view honest. A future calibration step can add a real target from a known fixture or motion reference.
